0.3.0: status colours keep their meaning, and Inspector Tabs follows your era.

- Colours that show a state, such as need bars, health, mood, skill levels and goodwill, now keep the standard green. Before, they took the era's accent colour, so in eras with a gold or yellow accent a full need bar looked like a half-empty one. Title bars, buttons and selected items keep the era colour.
- UI Rethemed - Inspector Tabs now shows your era's palette and pattern in the colonist and animal tabs.
- Animal icons, cards and details headers in UI Rethemed - Animals and Wildlife no longer show the era pattern, since animals don't belong to an era.
