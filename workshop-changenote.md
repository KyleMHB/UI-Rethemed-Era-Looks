0.2.0: new era patterns and a new settings page.

- The Architect, Animals, Colonist Card, History, Notifications, Quests and Wildlife windows now show your era's palette and pattern too, instead of the theme's slate and honeycomb.
- Every era now uses a flat material texture: fieldstone for animal, split wood for neolithic, cut stone for medieval, tread plate for industrial, hull panels for spacer, hex tiles for ultra and etched circuits for archotech. If you picked a pattern for an era yourself, your pick stays.
- Added four stone wall patterns to choose from: castle ashlar, coursed drystone, random rubble and levelled rubble.
- The settings page now uses UI Rethemed - Core's own cards and pickers, with a preview and palette swatches for each tech level.
- Patterns are drawn smaller on icons, tabs, buttons and cards so more of each one shows.
- Show each faction's era is now off by default, since a faction in another era's look can be mistaken for a change to your colony's look. This also turns it off if you had it on. Turn it on again in the Factions card in the settings.
- Spacer now has its own deep blue and cyan palette, and Ultra changes from teal and cyan to plum and magenta, so the two eras no longer look alike. Industrial changes from bottle green and brass to iron grey and steel. If you picked a palette for any of these eras yourself, your pick stays.
- Fixed the selected faction's Settlements and Overview graphs and leader card in UI Rethemed - Factions showing your colony's era instead of the faction's.
