# UI Rethemed - Era Looks

An addon for Lazy Friday Studio's UI Rethemed mods that gives the interface a look matching the colony's tech era, so a tribal or medieval colony isn't framed in spacer-style UI.

## Language

**Era**:
The colony's current tech level, as raised by research progression mods.
_Avoid_: Age, stage, tier

**Era look**:
The pair of a palette and a pattern that belongs to one tech level. Each of the seven levels, Animal through Archotech, has its own. The player can choose either half for each level; by default it is the look made for that level.
_Avoid_: Theme, skin, era theme

**Era texture**:
The plain material pattern of an era look: split wood, fieldstone, cut stone, tread plate, hull panels, hex tiles or etched circuits. Each is flat and white on clear, either adapted from a vanilla or DLC floor or building texture or drawn in that style, with no bevel or shading, in the style of UI Rethemed's own patterns. It suggests the era without showing its people or objects.
_Avoid_: Motifs, icons, decals

**Figure pattern**:
One of the older patterns made of an era's own artwork: rock art, tapestry and engraving. They are no longer any level's default but stay in the pattern lists.
_Avoid_: Era figures

**Default look**:
LFS's own slate palette and honeycomb pattern, Core's default pick. No era look uses it by default. The Ultra look has its own hex tiles, taken from the Ideology hex tile floor.
_Avoid_: Spacer look

**Follow the era**:
The player's choice to have the era look replace their own LFS Core pick while a game is loaded.
_Avoid_: Auto-theme, era mode

**Own pick**:
The palette and pattern a player chose in LFS Core's settings, shown whenever Follow the era is off or no game is loaded.
_Avoid_: Manual theme, user theme
